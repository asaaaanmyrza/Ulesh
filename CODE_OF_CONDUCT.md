
# Ulesh — Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Ulesh is an open-source initiative designed to connect restaurants with charitable organizations in Kazakhstan and reduce food waste through responsible food redistribution.

We aim to create a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment for project contributors, restaurants, charitable organizations, and future platform users.

We oppose discrimination, harassment, and behavior that may cause physical, social, or digital harm.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Treat others fairly, regardless of background, nationality, religion, gender, disability, or other personal characteristics.
- Respect different opinions and constructive feedback.
- Protect confidential and personal information.
- Report potentially unsafe or abusive behavior.
- Act honestly when providing information about food donations.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not permitted:

- Harassment, bullying, threats, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without authorization.
- Deliberately providing false or misleading information.
- Misusing the platform for fraud or exploitation.
- Offering food known to be unsafe for consumption.
- Ignoring food safety requirements during handling or redistribution.

## 4. Food Safety and Do No Harm

Ulesh is intended to facilitate the redistribution of surplus food that is still safe for human consumption.

The project proposes the following safety principles:

- Restaurants must provide accurate information about food type, quantity, expiration date, and relevant storage requirements.
- Food must not be offered if it is expired, contaminated, improperly stored, or otherwise unsafe.
- Relevant allergen information should be provided when applicable.
- Participating organizations must follow applicable food safety and hygiene requirements.
- Receiving organizations should inspect donations and may reject food that appears unsafe.
- Unsafe listings or suspected safety incidents should be reported and reviewed.

Ulesh is a coordination platform, not a food inspection authority. The responsibility of participating organizations and the need for compliance with applicable food safety laws remain essential.

## 5. Non-Discrimination and Fair Access

Ulesh aims to provide fair opportunities for eligible restaurants and charitable organizations to participate.

Participation decisions should be based on legitimate operational and safety requirements, not discriminatory preferences.

The project also aims to make its future interface accessible and understandable to different user groups.

## 6. Reporting Violations

Contributors and future users should be able to report harassment, discrimination, privacy violations, and unsafe food listings.

Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainers through a designated reporting channel. Sensitive personal information should not be posted in public GitHub Issues.

Before external users join the platform, the team must publish a working private reporting contact and define how reports will be reviewed.

## 7. Enforcement

The project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reported violations within the open-source community.

Depending on the severity of a violation, they may issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation.

Future platform-level reporting, account restrictions, and moderation procedures will be designed before deployment.

## 8. Project Status

Ulesh is currently in the planning and documentation stage.

This Code of Conduct establishes the project's intended ethical and safety principles. Automated moderation and platform-level safety enforcement have not yet been implemented.
